Don't play these

In chess notation, a question mark means a bad move. These are the three most common in this opening.

Bxf7+?

Taking on f7 too soon. You give a bishop for one pawn, and Black's king just steps away.

Qh5?

Bringing the queen out early. Black chases it with ...Nf6 and develops for free.

No O-O?

Waiting to castle. The center opens fast here, and a king on e1 gets caught in it.
