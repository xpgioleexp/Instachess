Check. The knight attacks the king on e1.

No capture. The bishop on e2 can't take it. It's pinned: move it and the queen on e4 hits the king.

No escape. d1, d2, f1 and f2 are all blocked by White's own pieces.

The lesson: after 3...Nd4, don't take on e5. Play 4.Nxd4 or castle.
