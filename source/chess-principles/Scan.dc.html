Strong players check these three things, in this order, every single turn:

1
Checks
Can you check the king? Forcing moves come first.
2
Captures
Can you win material? Is anything left unprotected?
3
Threats
What does your move attack, and what is your opponent planning?

Then run the same scan on your opponent's last move.
